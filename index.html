Our Story

From Ikeja to All of Nigeria

Philip Gadgets was founded over 12 years ago by Philip Isah J., starting small on the streets of Ikeja — Nigeria's heartbeat of tech commerce.

What began as a single-city operation has grown into a nationwide name trusted by over 5,000 buyers across all 36 states and beyond Nigerian borders. We built this business on one promise: deliver genuine gadgets at honest prices, with service that keeps customers coming back.

Today, Philip Gadgets primarily operates online — reaching you wherever you are. Our two physical touchpoints in Abule Egba (Pleasure bus stop) and Computer Village, Ikeja remain as testaments to where we started. We sell both brand new sealed devices and UK used phones in excellent condition.

  • Verified genuine products — no counterfeits, ever
  • Brand new & UK used excellent phones available
  • 12 years of industry expertise and trusted relationships
  • Serving customers from Lagos to Abuja, Kano, Port Harcourt and abroad
  • WhatsApp-driven, customer-first shopping experience
